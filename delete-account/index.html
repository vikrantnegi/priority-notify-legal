Account deletion

Delete your account.

Deleting your Priority Notify account removes everything tied to it. Nobody can reach you through the app afterwards.

In the app

  1. Open Priority Notify and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your data is deleted immediately and the app starts over.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email vikrantnegi007@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Priority Notify account" and your @handle. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your handle, display name and account identifier.
  • All your connections, in both directions, with their limits, quiet hours and blocks.
  • Every message you sent or received, and their delivery records.
  • Your phones' push tokens and device details, and any invite codes.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on purpose. Our hosting provider's encrypted backups expire on their own schedule. Your handle becomes free and someone else may claim it later.
